THREE BREATHS BACK TO THE PRESENT

How to draw a fortune for this moment

A meaningful draw begins by letting the question settle.

I

One honest question

Narrow your attention to the one thing that matters most right now. Honesty makes your own voice easier to hear.

II

Three quiet breaths

Breathe slowly three times. Do not assume good or bad, and do not keep drawing for the answer you want.

III

Find the next step

Treat the reading as a mirror. Keep the line that moves you, then choose one small action of your own.

Where do these readings come from?

Every lot is independently translated and condensed from the Chinese reference published by Wujia Longcheng Temple. This website is not affiliated with or endorsed by the temple.

Should I draw repeatedly for the same question?

It is usually more helpful to live with the first reading for a while. If the question and circumstances have not changed, repeated draws may only amplify anxiety.
