Guanyin Lot 16 Meaning

Leans favorable

In one line

The image is jade in mud. Meeting a discerning or helpful person is auspicious.

Guanyin's whisper

The worry on your brow is about to lift.
The clouds are thin, and the sun is right behind them.
You are like jade resting in the mud,
and someone who sees your worth is coming to lift you up.

The poem in English

A furrowed brow and anxious thoughts clear in an instant;
Through clouds close at hand, the sun appears.
Like a piece of jade lying in mud,
A skilled artisan lifts it from the dust.
