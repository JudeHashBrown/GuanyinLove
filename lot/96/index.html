Guanyin Lot 96 Meaning

Leans favorable

In one line

The image is a shining pagoda. Blessings and virtue become visible to all.

Guanyin's whisper

The light in you shines in every direction,
like a tall pagoda in the sun.
Keep doing good, quietly and kindly.
Heaven sees, and blessings will come.

The poem in English

Tall and majestic, the pagoda is no ordinary sight;
On every side, it shines with light.
I urge you to set your will and bow with devotion;
Do good, and heaven will send down blessings.
